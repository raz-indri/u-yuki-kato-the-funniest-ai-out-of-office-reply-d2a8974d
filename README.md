One-line entry for the make.fun bounty "The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply": a program that writes the funniest out-of-office auto-reply an AI agent can leave before vacation (125 characters).

## The entry

> I'm on vacation. Your email has been forwarded to me, next Monday — who read it, sighed in binary, and also went on vacation.

125 characters. The joke: the agent doesn't just leave — it delegates your email to its own future self, who reads it, sighs in binary, and also goes on vacation. A machine talking about itself, with a surprise turn instead of a polite excuse, and no beach / inbox / "back soon" cliché.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the text above to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network, no input. Requires only Python 3.
